A letter for you

Dear Anu,

“6 saal pehle main tumhe sirf door se dekhta tha… aaj 8 mahine se tum meri zindagi ka woh hissa ho jise main khona nahi chahta.”

Happy Birthday meri pyaari churail. ❤️

Ye tumhara first birthday hai jise main tumhare saath celebrate kar raha hoon, aur main chahta tha ke is baar wish sirf ek message na ho. Isliye ye chhoti si duniya tumhare liye banayi.

Main tumhare liye ek acha future, acha maqam, bohat saari khushiyan aur sukoon wish karta hoon. Tum apne har dream tak pohoncho, hamesha khush raho, aur tumhari smile kabhi kam na ho.

Thank you for loving me, meri care karne ke liye, larayi mein bhi mera khayal rakhne ke liye, mujhe tang karne ke liye, aur kabhi kabhi itni flirting karne ke liye ke mujhe samajh nahi aata ke main hansun ya sharmaun. 😭

Bas ek baat: meri 1 number aurat, aise hi rehna. Thori si pagal, thori si cute, thori si churail… aur meri favorite. 🤍

Happy Birthday, Anu. I hope this is one of those birthdays you remember for a very, very long time.

— Tumhara, [APNA NAAM YAHAN LIKHO]
